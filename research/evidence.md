## Methodological questions

- Does an observed association survive adjustment for plausible confounding and selection mechanisms?
- Does an experimental model reproduce a transient symptom or a long-term disease process?
- Are differences across studies attributable to populations, outcome definitions, timing, or analytical choices?
- Is a proposed mechanistic link supported by longitudinal or interventional evidence, or only by cross-sectional association?

## Illustrative critical question

Acute sleep deprivation may produce transient psychiatric-like symptoms. Such findings may offer clues about symptom generation, but they do not by themselves establish the origins of chronic psychiatric disorders. This is a methodological distinction, **not a completed empirical finding of this project**.

## Known limitations

- Exploratory scope and incomplete provenance verification
- AI-assisted classification requiring manual validation
- No verified classification agreement rate
- No peer-reviewed findings or demonstrated causal mechanism
