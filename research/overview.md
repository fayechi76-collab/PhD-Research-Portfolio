## Research question

How can potentially shared biological pathways across neuropsychiatric disorders be evaluated without confusing correlation, symptom induction, and disease causation?

## Exploratory domains

- Neuroimmune and inflammatory signalling
- Neuroendocrine and stress-response regulation
- Metabolic pathways
- Neural plasticity and network function

These are **categories for investigation**, not established common causes.

## Researcher role

The researcher defines questions and conceptual boundaries, critically examines methodological assumptions, and assesses competing interpretations. AI tools assist with exploration and organisation; independent validation remains pending.
