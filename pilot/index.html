Reviews and evidence synthesis

Systematic, narrative, scoping and methodological reviews, and all types of meta-analysis.

Included methods: Systematic review; Meta-analysis; Individual participant data meta-analysis; Network meta-analysis; Scoping review; Umbrella review; Narrative review; Review (subtype unconfirmed); Methodological review

Randomized controlled and crossover interventions

Randomized controlled and crossover designs remain distinct. Crossover design alone does not guarantee random allocation.

Included methods: Randomized controlled trial; Crossover intervention trial

Other human interventions and case studies

Human interventions with unconfirmed randomization, single cases and case series are kept separate from randomized trials.

Included methods: Controlled human intervention (randomization unconfirmed); Human experiment (randomization unconfirmed); Single case report; Case series

Human observational studies, genetic associations and secondary analyses

Cohort, cross-sectional, case-control, registry, genetic association and secondary-data studies. These cannot be directly interpreted as causal evidence.

Included methods: Prospective cohort study; Retrospective study; Longitudinal or repeated-measures study; Case-control study; Cross-sectional study; Registry-based study; Human observational study (specific design requires review); Genome-wide association study; Genetic association analysis; Gene-environment interaction analysis; Epigenetic analysis; Gene expression or transcriptomic analysis; Secondary analysis of existing data

Mendelian randomization

Studies that actually perform genetic instrumental-variable inference. Tutorials are excluded; causal interpretation depends on instrumental-variable assumptions.

Included methods: Mendelian randomization analysis

Animal, cell and ex vivo tissue studies

Animal interventions, cell cultures, ex vivo and postmortem tissue, and microbiota transfer. Species and experimental levels remain distinct.

Included methods: Animal intervention experiment; Animal tissue study; In vitro cell experiment; Ex vivo tissue study; Human postmortem tissue study; Microbiota transfer experiment

Methods, computational models, guidance and protocols

Method validation, computational and statistical analyses, guidelines, consensus, theoretical frameworks and protocols. Includes non-original research material and must not be treated as efficacy trials.

Included methods: Diagnostic accuracy study; Method validation or replication study; Methodological review; Methods guideline or tutorial; Clinical guideline; Clinical or methodological consensus; Commission report; Conceptual or theoretical framework; Study protocol or registry record; Editorial; Commentary or response; Book or textbook (no single study design applies); News report; Statistical network analysis; Computational modeling or simulation; Machine learning analysis; Mediation analysis; Bibliometric analysis

No records match the current filters. This does not imply that no related research exists worldwide.
