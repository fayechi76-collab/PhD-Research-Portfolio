# Independent Research Portfolio

## Cross-Diagnostic Biological Mechanisms of Neuropsychiatric Disorders

**Research type:** Self-directed, unfunded research without institutional affiliation  
**Period:** September 2026–present  
**Status:** Exploratory; no peer-reviewed publication to date

### Research focus

This project explores how biological mechanisms described across neuropsychiatric disorders may overlap, diverge, or interact. Areas of interest include neuroinflammation, neuroendocrine regulation, metabolism, and neural plasticity. The aim is to distinguish plausible mechanistic links from associations that do not establish causation.

### Literature-screening work

A large bibliographic dataset (approximately 93,000 records, **count and retrieval provenance pending verification**) has been assembled for literature screening and preliminary mechanism-oriented classification. This figure refers to bibliographic records, **not** full-text papers individually reviewed or mechanisms independently validated. Database, search dates, search strings, deduplication, screening stages, and classification coverage will be documented after verification against source logs.

### Researcher contributions

- Formulated research questions and defined the exploratory scope.
- Proposed a conceptual framework for comparing mechanisms across diagnostic categories.
- Critically examined causal claims, potential confounding, and the distinction between symptom induction and disease development.
- Identified methodological limitations requiring further validation.

### AI-assisted workflow and limitations

AI tools have supported literature exploration, preliminary categorisation, evidence organisation, methodological discussion, and technical development. These outputs should not be interpreted as independently validated systematic-review findings. Classification agreement and validation sample sizes have not yet been established. The researcher's role is in framing questions and critically assessing interpretations; AI-assisted work is explicitly disclosed.
